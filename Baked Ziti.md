**Baked Ziti \- Small Version**

A smaller version of the standard baked ziti with three assumptions:

**Specific Dish**: 6.5" X 10" X 1.75" Le Creuset \- 1400ml capacity  
**Béchamel and Marinara** : Already prepared and available

**Bechamel/Mornay Sauce and Pasta**

* 175g dried ziti/pasta  
* 2 cups Béchamel Sauce  
* 50g parmesan cheese  
* 75g shredded mozzarella  
* 100g frozen spinach


**Marinara Top Layer**

* 2 cups Marinara Sauce  
* 50g parmesan  
* 6 fresh mozzarella rounds

**Prep  \-**  Set the frozen spinach out and let it come up to room temp in a large, heatproof bowl.  Have some warm Béchamel and Marinara prepped and ready.  Start a medium pot with salted water and bring to a boil.  If you just made the Béchamel, remove from heat and set aside but leave it in the saucepan.

**Pasta and Mornay Sauce \-** When the water begins to boil, add the pasta, stirring occasionally and cook for 5 minutes.  It will be undercooked.   

In the bowl/saucepan with the warm Béchamel, add the parmesan and mozzarella and stir until melted to make your Mornay sauce.  Pour this over the room temp spinach and fold to combine.  When the pasta is done, strain and fold into the Mornay/Spinach sauce.  

**Assembly and Baking \-** Pour the pasta/Mornay/spinach mixture into your baking dish, spread the Marinara sauce over the top and cook covered with foil at 350  After 30 min, remove foil, add the parmesan and mozzarella rounds and cook for an additional 5 minutes.

 

**Baked Ziti v20.4**

**Marinara Sauce**

* 2 tbs olive oil  
* 20g  cloves garlic  
* 28 oz whole or crushed tomatoes  
* 150g diced fresh tomatoes  
* 2 tsp fresh oregano  
* 25g  minced fresh basil  
* 2 tsp sugar

**White Sauce**

* 350g ziti/penne (dried)  
* 100g frozen chopped spinach  
* 2 cup heavy cream  
* 2 tsp cornstarch  
* 2 eggs  
* 100g parmesan cheese  
* 450g cottage cheese  
* 150g mozzarella cheese  
* 1/4 cup basil  
* 50-100g extra moz cheese

**Marinara**  \- In a cold pan under medium heat add the olive oil and garlic. Cook until garlic just begins to brown and becomes aromatic. Add the canned tomatoes, diced tomatoes and oregano. Simmer for about 10 min stirring occasionally. Remove from heat, add basil, sugar, stir and set aside.  ALTERNATIVE – Use 800g Victoria Marinara sauce and ½ cup minced basil.

**Ziti \-**  Break up the frozen spinach in a colander.   Cook ziti only 5 minutes (yes, undercook it), pour the hot ziti and water over the spinach to thaw it.  

**White Sauce** \- In a separate bowl, beat the eggs; add the parmesan cheese, cottage cheese and half the mozzarella together. Heat cream and cornstarch on medium heat in the pan you cooked the ziti. When the mixture thickens (about 3-5 minutes) add in the egg/cheese mixture and turn off heat. Stir to incorporate.

**Assembly and Baking** \- Empty the colander full of ziti and spinach back in the white sauce and fold gently to combine. Pour into a 13" X 9" lasagna pan. Cover with the cooling tomato sauce.  

Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes. Remove foil, add cheese  and bake for another 15 min to let the cheese brown.  If you’re skilled, you can broil it momentarily at the end.
